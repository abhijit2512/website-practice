=====================================================
  DigitTech Website — How to upload & test it
=====================================================

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html        Home page
  about.html        About Us
  services.html     All 9 services
  internship.html   Internship & Training (with application form)
  contact.html      Contact (form, phone, WhatsApp, map)
  assets/           Logo, styles, 3D animation scripts (keep this folder next to the pages)

-----------------------------------------------------
1) QUICK LOOK ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
-----------------------------------------------------
  Unzip the file, then double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
  Everything works offline except the Google map and fonts (they need internet).

-----------------------------------------------------
2) TEST PRIVATELY ON YOUR HOSTING (before going live)  <-- recommended
-----------------------------------------------------
  This lets you check the site on your real domain WITHOUT replacing your current site.

  a) Log in to your hosting control panel (cPanel / hPanel).
  b) Open "File Manager" and go into the "public_html" folder.
  c) Create a new folder named:  preview
  d) Open "preview", click "Upload", and upload digittech-website.zip
  e) Right-click the zip, then choose "Extract". Make sure index.html sits directly inside "preview".
  f) Visit:  https://digittech.in/preview/
     Check it on your phone and computer.

-----------------------------------------------------
3) GO LIVE
-----------------------------------------------------
  OPTION A — Hosting WITHOUT WordPress (simplest)
    Upload & extract the zip directly in "public_html" (index.html directly inside it).
    Visit https://digittech.in  — done.

  OPTION B — Your domain currently runs WordPress
    This website is a fast, custom-coded site (plain HTML). It does NOT need WordPress.
    - Back up first: in File Manager, download a copy of public_html (or use your host's backup tool).
    - Upload & extract the zip into public_html. On most hosts index.html opens before
      WordPress's index.php, so the new site appears on your homepage.
    - If WordPress still shows, rename "index.php" in public_html to "index-wp-old.php".
    - To undo, delete index.html (or rename index-wp-old.php back to index.php).

    Prefer to keep it inside WordPress? Install the free "Insert Headers and Footers"
    or a "static HTML" page plugin. It works, but it's slower and harder to keep looking
    exactly like this. Option A/B is recommended.
